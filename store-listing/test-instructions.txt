No login needed.
1. Toolbar icon > Settings (gear): "Park windows after" > Custom, 1 minute, press Enter.
2. Open a second window with a web page; return to the first.
3. Within 2 minutes the second window shows a parking page. Focus it for 2 seconds (or click Restore tab): the tab returns.
Pinned, audible, protected or excluded tabs keep a window awake.
Right-click a page: Protect this tab, Exclude this site, Pause automatic parking.
Sync is off by default. Nothing goes to the developer.
